
## 🚀 Getting Started

1. **Download** or clone this repository.
2. Open `index.html` in your favorite web browser.
3. Start adding your cute tasks! 🎀

No build steps, no dependencies — just open and use!

## 📝 How to Use

1. **Add a task**: Type your task in the input field and click "Add task" or press Enter.
2. **Complete a task**: Click the checkbox next to any task to mark it as done.
3. **Delete a task**: Click the 🗑️ trash icon to remove a task.
4. **Filter tasks**: Use the filter buttons (All ✨ / Active 🧸 / Completed ✔️) to view specific tasks.
5. **Clear completed**: Click "Clear completed 🧹" to remove all finished tasks at once.

## 🧸 Example Tasks

The app comes pre-loaded with some cute examples:
- 🌸 water the plants
- 📖 read 10 pages
- ☕ enjoy a cozy break

Feel free to delete or modify them!

## 💾 Data Persistence

Your tasks are automatically saved in your browser's localStorage. Even if you close the tab or refresh the page, your to-do list will be waiting for you when you return.

## 📱 Responsive Design

The app is fully responsive and works beautifully on:
